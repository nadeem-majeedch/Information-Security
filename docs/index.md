# Information Security

**A self-study course from beginner to advanced** — 19 modules across 4 progressive levels, free tools, local labs, and a safety-first approach. Study on your own schedule, prove your skills through a personal portfolio of evidence, and finish with an integrated capstone. Everything in the course is free; nothing paid is ever required.

*Course materials by Engr. Dr. Muhammad Nadeem Majeed, Professor, Department of Data Science (PUCIT), University of the Punjab, Lahore, Pakistan.* Full details on the [About the Course](./about-the-course.md) page.

## Start here

1. **[Prerequisites](./prerequisites.md)** — check your setup (any modern computer, $0 budget, 8–12 h/week).
2. **[Lab Safety Policy](./lab-safety-policy.md)** — read and acknowledge before any hands-on work.
3. **[Module 01 — Foundations](./modules/module-01-information-security-foundations/README.md)** — begin the pathway.

## Course overview and learning outcomes

By the end of the course you can reason about risk with recognized frameworks, defend and harden systems, use cryptography correctly, test web applications safely against local targets, engineer detections mapped to MITRE ATT&CK, respond to incidents, and operate at program level — privacy, cloud, and automation — synthesized in a five-stage capstone. The full, measurable statements, each mapped to the modules that assess them, are in **[Course Learning Outcomes](./learning-outcomes.md)**; the complete curriculum with per-module outcomes, labs, and references is in the **[Course Roadmap](./course-roadmap.md)**; and the **[Course Completion Matrix](./course-completion-matrix.md)** shows, per module and outcome, what is published today (full lessons vs specification-and-labs stages).

## Capstone and your portfolio

The course ends with an **[Integrated Capstone Project](./capstone/README.md)**: two alternative scenario briefs, a seven-stage workflow with milestone gates, ready-made templates, and a transparent rubric. Your work across the course builds a personal **portfolio of evidence** — labs, quizzes, investigations, and deliverables you keep and can show others.

- Portfolio evidence rules: [Labs — Evidence and portfolio](./labs/README.md#evidence-and-portfolio)
